# THE GAMEVERSE — Storyboard (Phase 1)

Default total ≈ 24 s. Every beat lives in `src/config/experience.config.ts`. Skip works in every scene.

| # | Scene | Time (s) | Camera state | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | QR entry / loading | until ready | none | "INITIALIZING THE GAMEVERSE..." with an indeterminate bar and a real count of loaded items. Games data fetched. No Start button |
| 01 | Entering the chamber | 0 – 4.5 | Opening Chamber → Portal Reveal | Fog lifts from black, low wide shot. Monolith silhouettes and suspended fragments emerge; the dormant portal grows in frame |
| 02 | The portal awakens | 4.5 – 9.5 | Portal Activation | Pulse, particles drift inward, gold inscriptions light one by one, energy runs around the rings, fragments lean in, the surface starts to glow and the floor catches the light |
| 03 | Worlds behind the portal | 9.5 – 15.5 | World Previews | The surface shows each realm. One Brain: its real puzzle domains (logic, memory, pattern, spatial, word) with real screenshots, each with its own colour and pattern. Realm label from real data |
| 04 | Message | 15.5 – 19 | Message Reveal | "EVERY WORLD HAS A STORY." then "EVERY GAME IS A NEW ADVENTURE." placed above/below the portal |
| 05 | Full power | 19 – 21 | Portal Approach | Energy and particles surge, camera pushes in, the surface shows depth |
| 06 | Entering the portal | 21 – 24 | Portal Entry → Portfolio Handoff | Camera aligns with the opening and passes through the surface. A tunnel of energy fills the view and resolves into the portfolio background `#04050a` |
| 07 | Portfolio reveal | 24 | — | Real navigation to `https://ahshika.github.io/games/`, whose own menu entrance plays |

## Reduced motion

No camera flight and no flashing: the portal is shown active with the messages for a few seconds, then the same hand-off.

## Return (back button)

"THE GAMEVERSE / ENTER THE REALMS. DISCOVER THE GAMES." with **Enter the games** and **Watch again**. The intro is never forced again.
